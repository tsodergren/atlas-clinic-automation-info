**Effective date: October 27, 2026**

## Scope

Atlas Clinic Automation is a private application used only by Atlas Internal
Medicine and its authorized administrator. It is not offered to patients,
customers, or the general public.

## Information accessed

When authorized by the Atlas QuickBooks company administrator, the application
may access limited QuickBooks Online accounting data needed for verification and
remediation. This can include transaction identifiers, deposits, deposit lines,
dates, amounts, account references, payment-method references, and customer or
sub-customer references already stored in QuickBooks.

The application requests the QuickBooks Online Accounting permission only. It
does not request QuickBooks Payments or Payroll access.

## How information is used

Information is used only to:

- identify specific historical deposit lines selected for remediation;
- compare those lines with Atlas's protected local audit records;
- prepare and review proposed accounting changes;
- apply changes expressly approved by the Atlas administrator; and
- verify that accounting dates, accounts, splits, and amounts remain unchanged.

The application does not sell information, use it for advertising, create
consumer profiles, or provide it to data brokers.

## Storage and disclosure

Application credentials, authorization tokens, audit records, and temporary API
responses are maintained on an access-controlled Atlas workstation. They are not
stored in the public source-code repository or on this informational website.

Information is disclosed only as necessary to operate the authorized connection
with Intuit or as required by law. Intuit processes information under its own
terms and privacy statement.

## Retention and deletion

Atlas retains accounting audit records as needed for legitimate business,
compliance, and recordkeeping purposes. Temporary API responses and credentials
that are no longer required are securely deleted. QuickBooks authorization can
be revoked by the Atlas company administrator at any time.

## Security

Atlas limits access to authorized personnel and uses reasonable administrative
and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information.
Security credentials and authorization tokens are treated as confidential and
are excluded from source control and ordinary application logs.

## Questions and requests

Because this is an internal application with no public users, privacy questions
or requests should be directed to the Atlas Internal Medicine administrator who
provided access to the application.

This policy may be updated if the application's purpose, data handling, or legal
requirements change.
