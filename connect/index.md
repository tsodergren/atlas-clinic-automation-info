Atlas Clinic Automation is a private application and cannot be connected by the
public.

Connection or reconnection must be initiated locally by the authorized Atlas
Internal Medicine QuickBooks company administrator. The application requests the
QuickBooks Online Accounting permission only.

If authorization expires or is revoked, the Atlas administrator must repeat the
approved local authorization process. Credentials and authorization codes must
not be sent by email or entered into this website.
