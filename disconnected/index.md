The Atlas QuickBooks company administrator may disconnect Atlas Clinic
Automation at any time from QuickBooks Online's connected-app settings.

After disconnection, the application can no longer access QuickBooks through the
revoked authorization. Atlas will securely delete credentials and temporary API
responses that are no longer required, subject to legitimate accounting and
legal recordkeeping obligations.

This page does not itself disconnect the application or collect any information.
